## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system embodies an editorial agritech aesthetic: uncompromising farm provenance paired with rapid-commerce precision. It balances the urgency of immediate fulfillment with the artisanal dignity of agricultural origin. The interface feels organic yet sharp, modern yet deeply rooted in the soil. It avoids generic supermarket motifs in favor of editorial harvest journals, high-craft typography, and tactile produce presentation.

### Target Audience & Emotional Response
The target audience consists of quality-conscious urban households, conscious epicures, and modern consumers demanding radical transparency regarding origin, harvest timestamps, and grower compensation. The experience elicits feelings of harvest-morning freshness, uncompromising velocity, trust, and premium restraint.

### Visual Style Movement
The design movement is an organic hybrid of **Editorial Minimalism** and **Tactile Glassmorphism**. Layouts leverage generous warm whitespace, precise hairline borders, crisp provenance metadata tags, and soft, organic-tinted translucent surfaces that subtly refract rich soil and foliage tones beneath rapid-commerce interfaces.

## Colors

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#133E2B` - Deep Forest Green):** The institutional core representing vitality, depth, and rooted authority. Used for primary interactive actions, high-emphasis headings, key navigation chrome, and solid focus anchors.
- **Secondary (`#55B635` - Vibrant Leaf Lime):** The kinetic pulse representing crisp produce, immediate availability, and swift harvest. Applied to express delivery states, live inventory badges, harvest-time indicators, and success states.
- **Tertiary (`#8C5E3C` - Earthy Clay Brown):** Grounding soil warmth honoring growers and terroir. Applied to farm attribution labels, batch certification seals, harvest notes, and contextual editorial highlights.
- **Neutral Base (`#F9F8F3` - Warm Ivory Off-White):** Canvas baseline eliminating sterile clinical white in favor of natural daylight parchment.
- **Surface Elevation Tokens:**
  - `surface-canvas`: `#F9F8F3`
  - `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF`
  - `surface-muted`: `#F2EFE9`
  - `surface-glass`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` with deep forest border `rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.08)`
  - `text-primary`: `#0D281C` (ultra-deep pine black for high contrast readability)
  - `text-secondary`: `#4A5D52` (damp moss gray)
  - `text-muted`: `#7E8E84`

## Typography

### Structural Hierarchy
- **Headlines & Display (Sora):** Selected for its geometric stance, high x-height, and sculpted incisions that bridge agricultural integrity with modern logistical velocity. Headings are tight in line height, bold, and authoritative.
- **Body & Captions (DM Sans):** Chosen for pure legibility across catalog grids, nutritional panels, farm profiles, and checkout ledgers. High clarity at micro-sizes under variable mobile brightness.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase or small-caps styling used deliberately for harvest time stamps (e.g., `HARVESTED 4H AGO`), origin distances (`14 KM FROM HUB`), and farm tier badges.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture
- **Desktop/Large Tablet:** A 12-column fluid grid system bounded by a maximum container width of `1360px`. Column gutters hold at `1.5rem`, providing airy separation between discovery modules, harvest timelines, and product grids. Outer canvas margins hold at `2.5rem`.
- **Mobile/Handheld:** A 4-column adaptive layout with `1rem` outer canvas padding and `0.75rem` inner gutters to maximize screen density for fast multi-item cart building.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Built strictly around multiples of `0.25rem` (4px baseline). Element gaps inside micro-cards use `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while section breaks and catalog category divisions use `space-xl` and above.

## Elevation & Depth

### Philosophy: Atmospheric Farmstead Glass
Depth avoids synthetic dark drop-shadows, using instead botanical tinting, daylight ambient dispersion, and frosted surface isolation.

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Base canvas `#F9F8F3`. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Product Tiles & Produce Trays):** Background `#FFFFFF` with hairline border `1px solid rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.06)` and ambient shadow `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.04), 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Drawer, Flyout Cart & Hover States):** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, border `1px solid rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.09)`, and shadow `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Express Checkout Sheets, Sticky Harvest Navigation):** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(180%)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.60)`, and shadow `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

### Form Geometry
The shape language uses `roundedness: 2` (base `0.5rem` / 8px). It balances natural organic softness with industrial packaging precision:
- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges, Chips):** `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Cards & Category Panels (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) creates a friendly, tactile container reminiscent of modern produce crates and clean paper cartons.
- **Overlay Sheets & Hero Containers (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px) softens large editorial blocks.
- **Action Pills:** Full pill radius (`9999px`) is reserved exclusively for the instant cart addition stepper (`- 1 +`) and high-velocity conversion buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#133E2B`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Sora` semi-bold, rounded `9999px` (pill) for quick commerce actions or `0.5rem` for administrative triggers. Hover state shifts to `#1B4332` with an ambient glow of `rgba(85, 182, 53, 0.25)`.
- **Express / Accent Action:** Background `#55B635`, text `#0D281C` font weight 700. Used for "Harvest Now" or "15-Min Slot Locked".
- **Ghost / Tertiary Action:** Background transparent, text `#133E2B`, border `1px solid rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.18)`. Hover introduces `rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.04)` fill.

### Produce & Farmer Cards
- **Product Tiles:** Clean white surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.06)`, `1rem` border radius. Top section contains high-resolution produce photography on a softly tinted neutral-ivory backdrop. Top-right anchor hosts the live harvest badge (`e.g., 6h ago`). Bottom section features `Sora` pricing alongside farmer identification in clay brown (`#8C5E3C`), followed by a quick-add stepper button.
- **Farmer Profile Card:** Horizontal editorial split: portrait photography of the grower paired with soil metrics, fair-price guarantee percentage (`100% Direct Payout`), and geographic GPS coordinates.

### Chips & Tags
- **Harvest Tag:** Background `rgba(85, 182, 53, 0.12)`, text `#133E2B`, border `1px solid rgba(85, 182, 53, 0.3)`. Contains micro pulsing green dot indicator.
- **Terroir / Soil Tag:** Background `rgba(140, 94, 60, 0.10)`, text `#8C5E3C`, font weight 600, uppercase letter-spacing `0.05em`.
- **Speed Tag:** Background `#133E2B`, text `#84CC16` font weight 700.

### Input Fields & Search Discovery
- **Rapid Search Bar:** Frosted ivory surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`), border `1px solid rgba(19, 62, 43, 0.14)`, `9999px` pill geometry, height `52px`. Placeholder text set in `DM Sans` (`text-muted`). Focus state reveals crisp `#133E2B` outline with soft lime ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(85, 182, 53, 0.25)`).

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- Checkbox elements use a `0.375rem` rounded border in `#133E2B`. Active state fills `#133E2B` with a crisp ivory `#F9F8F3` checkmark icon.

### Live Route & Delivery Drawer
- Glassmorphic slide-over bottom sheet utilizing `surface-glass`, `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, tracking route progress from farm gate to consumer doorstep with animated lime-green pulse nodes.
